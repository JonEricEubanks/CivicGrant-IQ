SECTION 1: PROJECT DESCRIPTION
=============================================================================

1.1 Overview

The Village of Buffalo Grove requests $5,000,000 in RAISE grant funding to
reconstruct the Aptakisic Road / Illinois Route 83 intersection, the highest-
volume and highest-crash intersection within the Village's roadway network.
This project will deliver measurable safety improvements, reduce congestion,
enhance pedestrian and bicycle connectivity, and improve access to the Prairie
View Metra station (North Central Service Line) for approximately 41,000 Buffalo
Grove residents and an estimated 28,500 regional travelers who pass through this
intersection daily.

The project transforms a substandard, signal-controlled intersection into a
modern, multi-modal transportation hub with geometric improvements, protected
bike lanes, enhanced pedestrian crossings, and upgraded traffic signal technology.
Total reconstruction cost is $8.25 million, with $5 million requested from RAISE
and $3.25 million committed from local and state sources.

1.2 Problem Statement

The Aptakisic Road / IL-83 intersection is the most dangerous intersection in
Buffalo Grove and one of the highest-severity intersections in Lake County, IL.

Safety Data (IDOT Crash Records, 2019-2023):
  - Total crashes: 87 (5-year period)
  - Injury crashes: 31 (36% of total — significantly above statewide 22% average)
  - Fatal crashes: 2 (2021, 2022 — both involving pedestrians)
  - Crash rate: 1.42 crashes per million entering vehicles (statewide average: 0.68)
  - Critical Injury Rate: 0.52 (statewide average: 0.18)

The intersection currently experiences:
  - Average daily traffic (ADT): 28,400 vehicles (IDOT 2023 count)
  - AM peak hour queuing: 14 vehicles on Aptakisic westbound approach (LOS F)
  - PM peak hour volume: 3,840 vehicles/hour (exceeds design capacity by 22%)
  - Turn lane deficiencies: No dedicated northbound left-turn lane on IL-83
  - Sidewalk gaps: 0.6-mile gap on Aptakisic Road east of the intersection
  - Bicycle facility: None — nearest protected lane is 2.1 miles away
  - Bus stop: Pace Route 574 stops without shelter or ADA landing pad

The 2022 IL-83 Corridor Safety Study (IDOT District 1) specifically identified
this intersection as a High-Injury Network location and recommended geometric
reconstruction as the only effective long-term countermeasure.

1.3 Project Scope

COMPONENT A — Geometric Reconstruction ($3.2M):
  - Add dedicated northbound and southbound left-turn lanes on IL-83 (dual left)
  - Extend westbound left-turn lane on Aptakisic Road from 75 feet to 250 feet
  - Add exclusive right-turn lane on Aptakisic Road eastbound approach
  - Restripe all approaches to 12-foot lanes per AASHTO standards
  - Replace all four intersection radii with 35-foot radius returns (from 20-foot)
  - Install concrete pedestrian refuge island in all four crosswalk locations

COMPONENT B — Signal Modernization ($890,000):
  - Replace 1998 signal controller with Adaptive Traffic Signal Control (ATSC)
  - Install connected vehicle (V2I) transponders for emergency vehicle preemption
  - Upgrade pedestrian signal heads to audible/countdown pedestrian signals (APS)
  - Install bicycle detection loops on all approaches
  - Connect to Lake County Traffic Signal Management System (TSMS)

COMPONENT C — Pedestrian & ADA Improvements ($680,000):
  - Reconstruct all 8 pedestrian curb ramps to 2024 PROWAG standards
  - Install high-visibility continental crosswalk markings (4-inch thermoplastic)
  - Construct 0.6-mile sidewalk connection to Aptakisic Road (east of intersection)
  - Install ADA-compliant bus stop shelter and landing pad for Pace Route 574
  - Install accessible pedestrian signal push buttons with locator tones

COMPONENT D — Protected Bicycle Facility ($580,000):
  - Install 1.8-mile protected bike lane connecting intersection to Prairie View
    Metra station (Class I separated facility per NACTO Urban Street Design Guide)
  - Install bike signal heads on IL-83 approaches
  - Install bicycle detection cameras on all approaches
  - Install "sharrow" markings on Aptakisic Road east connector to existing trail

COMPONENT E — Project Development and Construction Oversight ($900,000):
  - Final design (plans, specifications, estimates)
  - Environmental review (Categorical Exclusion — approved December 2023)
  - Right-of-way acquisition (2 partial takes from willing landowners — agreed in principle)
  - Construction engineering and inspection
  - Materials testing
  - As-built documentation and IDOT project closeout

SECTION 2: MERIT CRITERIA
=============================================================================

2.1 SAFETY

This project will prevent an estimated 12 injury crashes and 0.4 fatalities
annually based on Crash Modification Factors (CMFs) from the FHWA CMF Clearinghouse:
  - Left-turn lane addition: CMF 0.71 (29% crash reduction for turning crashes)
  - Adaptive signal control: CMF 0.83 (17% overall crash reduction)
  - Continental crosswalks: CMF 0.66 (34% reduction in pedestrian-vehicle conflicts)
  - Protected bike lane: CMF 0.44 (56% reduction in bike-vehicle crashes)

Benefit-Cost Analysis (preliminary, using USDOT BCA guidance):
  - Annualized safety benefit: $2,840,000 (VSL-based, $11.6M/fatality, $1.1M/serious injury)
  - Congestion reduction benefit: $890,000/year (2,200 hours/day × $51 value of time)
  - Total 20-year NPV benefit: $48.6M
  - Benefit-Cost Ratio (BCR): 5.9:1

The Aptakisic/IL-83 intersection is on Illinois's "High-Injury Network" (HIN),
a statutory designation under Illinois P.A. 102-0176 (Vision Zero Planning Act).
Buffalo Grove adopted its Complete Streets Policy in 2022 and committed to zero
traffic fatalities by 2030.

2.2 EQUITY

  - Pace Route 574 serves a corridor with 28% transit-dependent households
  - Project area is within CMAP's Equity Priority Community designation (EPC)
  - Bicycle connection serves the Prairie View Metra station, enabling car-free
    commuting for an estimated 340 additional households
  - Construction employment: Village commits to 25% hiring from Cook County and
    Lake County Department of Employment Security (IDES) priority zip codes
  - Community engagement: Three open houses (June-August 2024), translated notices
    in Spanish and Hindi (two largest non-English communities in BG)

2.3 ECONOMIC COMPETITIVENESS

  - The Lake-Cook Road corridor, accessible via this intersection, contains
    Buffalo Grove's primary commercial district: 2.8 million square feet of
    retail, office, and industrial space, supporting 8,200 jobs
  - The Prairie View Metra station (1.4 miles from the intersection) provides
    direct rail service to Chicago O'Hare International Airport and downtown
    Chicago's Union Station
  - The new protected bike lane will connect the intersection to the existing
    Buffalo Grove/Vernon Hills trail network (47 miles of trails)

2.4 SUSTAINABILITY

  - Adaptive signal control reduces vehicle idling, cutting CO2 emissions by an
    estimated 840 metric tons per year (EPA MOVES model, preliminary)
  - Project achieves LEED for Neighborhood Development (ND) credits for
    transportation access improvements
  - Native plantings in the right-of-way median (project Component A) support
    pollinators and reduce mowing maintenance by 40%
  - The pedestrian/bicycle component eliminates an estimated 180,000 vehicle-
    miles traveled (VMT) annually as Metra-bound commuters shift modes

2.5 MULTI-MODAL AND PARTNERSHIPS

Partners:
  - Illinois Department of Transportation District 1: Project champion; has
    committed $1,500,000 in STP-Urban funds and expedited permit review
  - Lake County Division of Transportation: Committed $750,000 toward signal
    communications integration with County TSMS
  - Pace Suburban Bus: Letter of support; will upgrade Route 574 service frequency
    after ADA improvements complete
  - CMAP: Transportation for Illinois Coalition endorsement
