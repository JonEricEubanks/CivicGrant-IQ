EPA CLEAN WATER STATE REVOLVING FUND (CWSRF) — ALL STATES
  Status: OPEN YEAR-ROUND (rolling applications through state EPA offices)
  What to do: Contact your state environmental agency TODAY; place project on priority list
  Illinois (IEPA): Priority list cycle — applications accepted January–March each year
    Next cycle: January 2027 for FY2027 funding (apply now for FY2026 late allocations)
  Texas (TCEQ): Rolling applications; CWSRF project priority list submitted quarterly
  California (SWRCB): Applications accepted year-round; priority list updated quarterly
  Average processing: 6-12 months from application to loan closing
  Note: This is the single most accessible infrastructure program in the US —
    no competition, no NOFO, apply through your state environmental agency.

EPA DRINKING WATER SRF — LEAD SERVICE LINE REPLACEMENT
  Status: OPEN YEAR-ROUND (rolling through state agencies)
  IIJA money: $15 billion over 5 years — states have not fully deployed this yet
  Priority: ANY water system with documented lead service lines should apply immediately
  Contact: State drinking water program (IEPA, TCEQ, CDPH, etc.)

USDA RURAL DEVELOPMENT — COMMUNITY FACILITIES

USDOT SAFE STREETS & ROADS FOR ALL (SS4A) — FY2026 NOFO
  Expected NOFO publication: July 2026 (historically published June-August)
  Expected application deadline: September–October 2026
  Award announcement: Spring 2027
  What to do NOW:
    1. Adopt a Vision Zero resolution at city council (required for Implementation grants)
    2. Compile 5-year crash data by location, type, and severity (KABCO scale)
    3. Map your High-Injury Network (top 5-10% of road segments with highest injury crash density)
    4. Identify highest-priority safety project with 30%+ design
    5. Draft public engagement plan for underserved populations
  Award range: $1M–$25M (Implementation); $100K–$5M (Action Plan)
  Note: SS4A Implementation grants have LOWER competition than RAISE — fewer applicants,
    same federal funding pool for safety projects.

SECTION 6: GRANT PREPARATION TIMELINE FOR RAISE (MODEL FOR ANY MAJOR GRANT)
=============================================================================

12 months before deadline (Month 1):
  → Identify project; confirm it aligns with program priorities
  → Commission feasibility/preliminary engineering study
  → Begin collecting crash/condition/usage data
  → Contact congressional delegation offices to notify of intent to apply

9 months before deadline (Month 3):
  → Commission BCA analysis
  → Begin NEPA environmental review
  → Secure local match commitment (city council resolution)
  → Begin community engagement documentation

6 months before deadline (Month 6):
  → Complete BCA; confirm BCR ≥ 1.5
  → Advance design to 30%+
  → Secure state agency support letters (state DOT, state emergency management)
  → Schedule congressional office visits; request support letters

3 months before deadline (Month 9):
  → Begin full application narrative writing
  → Compile all data tables, exhibits, photos
  → Request letters from partners (county, transit agency, MPO)
  → Internal review of draft application against NOFO evaluation criteria checklist

1 month before deadline (Month 11):
  → Final internal review
  → All certifications and assurances signed
  → Grants.gov submission test run
  → Backup plan if portal goes down (have printed copy + PDF ready)

1 week before deadline:
  → Submit early (Grants.gov crashes on deadline day — submit 1 week early)
  → Confirm submission receipt email from Grants.gov
  → Send courtesy notification to state program office
