VILLAGE OF BUFFALO GROVE, ILLINOIS
CAPITAL IMPROVEMENT PLAN — FY2026–FY2030
Adopted by Village Board: December 16, 2025

=============================================================================
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
=============================================================================
The Village of Buffalo Grove's 5-Year Capital Improvement Plan (CIP) for
FY2026–FY2030 totals $89.4 million across 47 projects. This document summarizes
the 15 highest-priority projects, their funding status, and grant opportunities
being actively pursued. The CIP was developed under direction of Village Manager
Dane Bragg and prepared by Public Works Director and Village Engineer Briget Schwab.

Village Profile (for grant context):
  Population: 41,496 (2020 Census)
  Incorporated Area: 9.6 square miles
  Median Household Income: $103,847
  Moody's Bond Rating: Aa2 (reaffirmed January 2025)
  General Fund Reserves: $14.6 million (18.7% of operating budget)
  Unreserved Capital Fund Balance: $15.4 million (available for project match)
  US News Ranking: #1 Best Place to Live in Illinois (2026-2027)

PROJECT CATEGORY 1: TRANSPORTATION
=============================================================================

PROJECT T-1: Aptakisic Road / IL-83 Intersection Reconstruction
Priority: CRITICAL | Status: Grant application submitted; ready to proceed
Total Cost: $8,250,000 | Grant Target: $5,000,000 RAISE (USDOT)
Local Committed: $1,000,000 | State Committed: $2,250,000 (IDOT STP-Urban + LCDOT)
Start: FY2026 (upon award) | Complete: FY2027

Problem: Highest-crash intersection in the Village (87 crashes/5 years; 2 fatalities).
ADT: 28,400 vehicles/day. LOS F in PM peak. No pedestrian/bicycle facilities.
Scope: Add dual left-turn lanes on IL-83, extend westbound storage, install
adaptive traffic signal, protected bike lane to Prairie View Metra station,
ADA pedestrian upgrades.
Grant Notes: RAISE FY2024 application submitted September 2024. If not awarded,
will reapply FY2026. IDOT IL-83 Corridor Safety Study recommends this project
as highest priority statewide. Also eligible for HSIP (IDOT Highway Safety Program).

---

PROJECT T-2: Lake-Cook Road Corridor Improvements (Village Segment)
Priority: HIGH | Status: Design 60% complete
Total Cost: $5,600,000 | Grant Target: $2,800,000 CMAQ/STP
Local Committed: $2,800,000
Start: FY2026 | Complete: FY2027

Problem: Lake-Cook Road (Village of Buffalo Grove segment, 1.2 miles) has
deteriorating pavement (PCI 32 — "Very Poor"), no bike lanes, and inadequate
intersection sight distance at Raupp Blvd. The corridor carries 22,400 VPD and
serves Buffalo Grove's primary commercial district.
Scope: Full-depth reclamation, lane restriping, new protected bike lanes (Class I),
three intersection geometric improvements, new LED street lighting, streetscape
improvements aligned with Village's 2024 Downtown Vision Plan.
Grant Notes: CMAP call for projects (STP) submitted 2024; anticipated award FY2026.
Also eligible for Illinois Transportation Enhancement Program (ITEP).

---

PROJECT T-3: Weiland Road Reconstruction (Phase II)
Priority: HIGH | Status: Phase I construction complete
Total Cost: $4,100,000 | Grant Target: $1,800,000 (STP-Urban)
Local Committed: $2,300,000
Start: FY2027 | Complete: FY2028

Problem: Weiland Road south of Lake-Cook Road (2,300 LF) has pavement condition
index of 28 ("Very Poor"); 12-inch water main installed 1971 is past service life;
sanitary sewer main has infiltration/inflow exceeding IEPA limits.
Scope: Full pavement reconstruction, water main replacement (16-inch), sewer
lining (CIPP), new pedestrian sidewalk on east side, ADA ramp upgrades.

---

PROJECT T-4: Long Grove Road Trail Connection
Priority: MEDIUM | Status: Alignment study complete
Total Cost: $1,850,000 | Grant Target: $1,480,000 ITEP/TAP
Local Committed: $370,000
Start: FY2027 | Complete: FY2028

Problem: 1.4-mile gap in the regional trail network between Buffalo Grove's
existing trail network and the Long Grove Forest Preserve trail entrance.
Scope: Construct 10-foot-wide concrete multi-use path along Long Grove Road
right-of-way, 3 pedestrian crossing improvements, 2 new wayfinding kiosks
integrated with regional trail map system.
Grant Notes: ITEP call for projects — next cycle opens FY2026. Strong project
given connectivity to preserve and proximity to Metra station.

---

PROJECT T-5: Prairie View Metra Station Area Improvements
Priority: HIGH | Status: Feasibility study complete; TOD master plan underway
Total Cost: $6,400,000 | Grant Target: $4,800,000 RAISE / BUILD / FTA
Local Committed: $1,600,000
Start: FY2028 | Complete: FY2030

Problem: Prairie View Metra station (North Central Service) serves Buffalo Grove
but has inadequate parking (347 spaces; avg daily occupancy 94%), no bike parking,
dangerous pedestrian access from the east, and no real-time transit information.
Daily Metra boardings: 1,240.
Scope: 200-space structured parking expansion, protected pedestrian path from
Aptakisic Road (linking to T-1 project), real-time transit information kiosks,
bike corral (40 spaces), electric vehicle charging stations (24 ports), plaza and
wayfinding improvements.
Grant Notes: Eligible for FTA RAISE and BUILD. Strong synergy with T-1 (Aptakisic
intersection) and T-4 (trail connection). Village has engaged Senator Durbin's
office re: FY2026 RAISE earmark consideration.

PROJECT CATEGORY 2: STORMWATER AND ENVIRONMENT
=============================================================================

PROJECT S-1: Northwood Neighborhood Stormwater Improvements (Phase I)
Priority: CRITICAL | Status: AWARDED — Construction Active
Total Cost: $13,800,000 | SMC SIIP Grant Awarded: $5,500,000
Local Match: $8,300,000 (Village $6,500,000; Park District $700,000; Vernon Twp $300,000; Water Fund $800,000)
Start: January 2025 | Complete: March 2026

Scope: Wetland naturalization (Northwood Park), Ridgewood Pond outlet, ditch
regrading and culvert replacement, Northwood Drive/Ridgewood Drive reconstruction.
See past grant application on file for full project description.
Note: Phase II (remaining 180 properties, estimated $6.2M) targeted for FY2028
with SMC SIIP Round 2 application.

---

PROJECT S-2: Buffalo Creek Flood Resilience — Early Warning + Green Infrastructure
Priority: HIGH | Status: BRIC application submitted January 2025; under review
Total Cost: $4,250,000 | FEMA BRIC Request: $3,400,000
Local Committed: $850,000
Start: FY2026 (upon award) | Complete: FY2027

Scope: 7 new stream gauges, 3 green infrastructure rain garden networks at
commercial sites (42 acres impervious), Weiland Lift Station hardening to
protect against 500-year flood.
See past grant application on file for full project description.

---

PROJECT S-3: Strathmore Neighborhood Drainage Improvements
Priority: HIGH | Status: Master plan complete; grant search active
Total Cost: $7,200,000 | Grant Target: $3,600,000 IEPA / SMC SIIP Round 2
Local Committed: $3,600,000
Start: FY2027 | Complete: FY2029

Problem: 189 properties in the Strathmore neighborhood (southwest Buffalo Grove)
experience flooding in 2-year storm events. The primary cause is an undersized
24-inch trunk main installed in 1969 that cannot convey design flows.
Scope: Replace 3,800 LF of 24-inch trunk main with 48-inch storm sewer, construct
2.4-acre regional detention basin on Village-owned open space, reline 5 existing
detention basins (CIPP).

---

PROJECT S-4: Green Alley Program — Phase I (Historic Downtown)
Priority: MEDIUM | Status: Pilot site identified; grant application planned
Total Cost: $920,000 | Grant Target: $650,000 CMAP / EPA 319(h)
Local Committed: $270,000
Start: FY2026 | Complete: FY2027

Problem: Six unimproved alleys in the historic business district generate
significant stormwater runoff and contribute to downstream loading of Buffalo
Creek with motor oil, heavy metals, and suspended sediment.
Scope: Convert 6 alleys (total 4,300 LF) to permeable pavement, install
bioretention strips, connect to municipal storm sewer system.

PROJECT CATEGORY 3: PARKS AND RECREATION
=============================================================================

PROJECT P-1: Clove Park Multi-Sport Complex
Priority: HIGH | Status: Community survey complete (78% support); design phase
Total Cost: $8,900,000 | Grant Target: $4,000,000 OSLAD / LWCF
Local Committed: $4,900,000
Start: FY2026 | Complete: FY2028

Scope: Conversion of outdated Clove Park athletic facilities into modern multi-
sport complex: four new synthetic turf multi-sport fields (replace asphalt 1970s
surfaces), two new pickleball courts, accessible playground (all-inclusive design),
expanded parking, stormwater capture system for field irrigation, new restroom/
concession building. Project will serve 14 youth and adult athletic leagues with
combined 4,200 registered participants.
Grant Notes: IL OSLAD call for projects — FY2026. Also eligible for national
LWCF program via IDNR. Buffalo Grove has not previously received OSLAD funding.

---

PROJECT P-2: Veterans Park Renovation
Priority: MEDIUM | Status: Design complete
Total Cost: $1,200,000 | Grant Target: $720,000 CDBG / OSLAD
Local Committed: $480,000
Start: FY2026 | Complete: FY2027

Scope: Complete renovation of Veterans Park: new ADA accessible paths, inclusive
playground equipment, pollinator garden, veterans memorial enhancements, new
irrigation system. Project is located in a census tract meeting HUD CDBG
benefit area requirements (51% LMI eligible).

---

PROJECT P-3: North Park Tennis Center Renovation
Priority: MEDIUM | Status: Concept design
Total Cost: $1,600,000 | Grant Target: $800,000 USTA / CDBG
Local Committed: $800,000
Start: FY2028 | Complete: FY2029

Scope: Replace 8 aging tennis courts (original 1982 construction), add 4 pickleball
overlay courts, new LED lighting, ADA accessible parking expansion, clubhouse HVAC

---

PROJECT U-2: Water Main Replacement — Phase I (Northwest District)
Priority: HIGH | Status: Condition assessment complete
Total Cost: $5,400,000 | Grant Target: $2,700,000 IEPA PWSLOAN / WIIA
Local Committed: $2,700,000
Start: FY2026 | Complete: FY2028

Problem: 4.8 miles of water main in the northwest district (installed 1966-1974)
have experienced 34 breaks in the past 5 years and test positive for elevated
lead service line connections at 18 residential properties.
Scope: Replace all 4.8 miles with 12-inch ductile iron pipe, replace 18 lead
service lines (full line replacement under LCWR requirements), install 12 new
fire hydrants, pressure-test and recommission.
Grant Notes: IEPA PWSLOAN program; also WIIA (Water Infrastructure Improvements
for the Nation Act) if lead service lines confirmed. BG has documented lead risk.

---

PROJECT U-3: Municipal Campus Energy Efficiency Upgrade
Priority: MEDIUM | Status: Energy audit complete
Total Cost: $2,100,000 | Grant Target: $1,050,000 ICLEI / IL Clean Energy
Local Committed: $1,050,000
Start: FY2027 | Complete: FY2028

Scope: HVAC replacement at Village Hall, Public Works garage, and Police HQ;
LED lighting conversion across all municipal buildings; solar PV installation
on Village Hall roof (180 kW); EV charging stations at municipal campus (12 ports).
Projected energy savings: 38% reduction in municipal energy costs ($280,000/year).
Grant Notes: ICLEI Green Streets grant; DCEO clean energy incentives; IRA
Section 179D deduction for government buildings (new under Inflation Reduction Act).
